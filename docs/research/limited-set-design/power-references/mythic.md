Fetched 2026-10-16. Bands are editorial judgments for the specified context. The card links establish printed facts, not our classification. Read the full costs and support requirements before applying a verdict.

## Creature

### Too Strong: [Uro, Titan of Nature's Wrath](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/229/uro-titan-of-natures-wrath)

**{1}{G}{U}; 6/6 · Legendary Creature — Elder Giant · THB #229 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** recursive value and ramp threat. **Effect summary:** Three mana gains life, draws, and can put a land into play; escape later retains a 6/6 that repeats the value when attacking.

**Why this band:** Conditional upper boundary: cheap stabilization and acceleration lead into a recurring threat, making one-for-one answers struggle to catch up. This is an engine package, not just a large creature.

**Costs and context:** Requires escape mana and five other graveyard cards for the persistent body. Graveyard hate and exile change its resilience; do not omit those costs.

### Target: [Archon of Cruelty](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/75/archon-of-cruelty)

**{6}{B}{B}; 6/6 · Creature — Archon · MH2 #75 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** expensive stabilizing finisher. **Effect summary:** An eight-mana 6/6 flyer creates a sacrifice, discard, life swing, and card draw on entry and attack.

**Why this band:** This is appropriate ambition for eight mana: immediate recovery plus a real closing threat. Do not flatten its payoff to a bare large body because the effect looks dramatic.

**Costs and context:** The opponent sacrifices a creature or planeswalker rather than a chosen target. Cheap reanimation or repeated blink can move this beyond the hard-cast reference context.

### Too Weak: [Sphinx Sovereign](https://scryfall.com/card/ala/196/sphinx-sovereign)

**{4}{W}{U}{U}{B}; 6/6 · Artifact Creature — Sphinx · ALA #196 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** expensive evasive threat. **Effect summary:** An eight-mana 6/6 flyer gains three life at your end step if untapped, otherwise makes opponents lose three life.

**Why this band:** Below the eight-mana mythic target: no entry card or removal swing, difficult colors, and most value requires surviving until a later step.

**Costs and context:** Can stabilize against flyers and eventually race. Compare immediate payoff and colored requirements with Archon rather than body size alone.

## Instant

### Too Strong: [Mana Drain](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/65/mana-drain)

**{U}{U} · Instant · IMA #65 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** counterspell plus mana swing. **Effect summary:** A two-blue counterspell supplies colorless mana equal to the countered spell's mana value at the next main phase.

**Why this band:** Above the desired interaction ceiling: a full answer also finances a much earlier threat. Increasing rarity changes frequency but does not remove the tempo swing.

**Costs and context:** Requires a relevant opposing spell, double blue, and a way to use the temporary colorless mana. Its uncommon and mythic printings demonstrate why rarity alone is not a power scale.

### Target: [Magma Opus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/203/magma-opus)

**{6}{U}{R} · Instant · STX #203 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** expensive flexible board and card swing. **Effect summary:** Eight mana combines divided damage, tapping permanents, a 4/4 token, and two cards; discarding it for two mana instead makes a Treasure.

**Why this band:** A suitable mythic spell offers several concrete returns when expensive and an early use when stuck in hand. This is the right scale for a late-game resource swing.

**Costs and context:** Costs eight at instant speed; the early Treasure mode spends the card. Casting from the graveyard cheaply would require separate package review.

### Too Weak: [Stunning Reversal](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/51/stunning-reversal)

**{3}{B} · Instant · BBD #51 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** emergency loss replacement. **Effect summary:** Four-mana instant replaces the next game loss that turn with drawing seven and setting life to one, then exiles itself.

**Why this band:** Weak default mythic slot: no development or ordinary interaction, must be held for a narrow crisis, and often leaves the player exposed at one life.

**Costs and context:** A special alternate-loss combo or protection plan can improve it. Do not credit that package when absent.

## Sorcery

### Too Strong: [Channel](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/157/channel)

**{G}{G} · Sorcery · IMA #157 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** life-funded fast mana. **Effect summary:** Two green mana enables paying life for colorless mana during the turn.

**Why this band:** Above the upper bound with expensive generic-cost payoffs: it can bypass most of the mana curve immediately. Moving it from uncommon to mythic does not make that play pattern fair.

**Costs and context:** Requires payoff cards and enough life. An empty hand or colored-only costs limit it; do not invent a combo without identifying the payoff.

### Target: [Echo of Eons](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/46/echo-of-eons)

**{4}{U}{U} · Sorcery · MH1 #46 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** graveyard-enabled hand reset. **Effect summary:** Six-mana spell shuffles hands and graveyards into libraries and draws seven for everyone; flashback costs three.

**Why this band:** A suitable high-powered build-around when its graveyard mode is deliberately supported: a meaningful hand reset at an attractive alternate price, with real symmetry and sequencing decisions.

**Costs and context:** Needs discard or mill to access the cheaper mode. Also refills the opponent; free discard and fast mana can make the package excessive. Not a general-purpose baseline draw spell.

### Too Weak: [Archangel's Light](https://scryfall.com/card/dka/1/archangels-light)

**{7}{W} · Sorcery · DKA #1 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** expensive life gain and graveyard reset. **Effect summary:** Eight mana gains two life per graveyard card, then shuffles that graveyard into the library.

**Why this band:** Too little board or usable card impact for an eight-mana mythic. A large life total can delay losing without providing a way to recover or win.

**Costs and context:** Life-gain payoffs and decking can matter; without them, compare it directly with a finisher that also removes, draws, or supplies bodies.

## Enchantment

### Too Strong: [Necropotence](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/98/necropotence)

**{B}{B}{B} · Enchantment · IMA #98 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** life-funded card engine. **Effect summary:** A three-black enchantment trades life for cards arriving at the end step, while skipping normal draws and exiling discarded cards.

**Why this band:** Above our sustained-card-advantage ceiling: many cards can be purchased for life without further mana, rapidly overwhelming ordinary exchanges.

**Costs and context:** Triple black, delayed access, hand size, skipped draws, and pressure on life are real constraints. They do not make life a substitute for a meaningful ongoing mana cost.

### Target: [The Meathook Massacre](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/112/the-meathook-massacre)

**{X}{B}{B} · Legendary Enchantment · MID #112 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** scalable sweeper and death engine. **Effect summary:** An X-cost enchantment shrinks all creatures on entry and then turns deaths into life swings.

**Why this band:** A strong mythic should affect the board immediately and leave a coherent engine. The sweeper scales with actual mana spent; the remaining triggers need creatures to die.

**Costs and context:** The shrink effect hits your creatures too. Small-creature density and token sacrifice can raise its ceiling; do not automatically label every death trigger excessive.

### Too Weak: [Cast Through Time](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/55/cast-through-time)

**{4}{U}{U}{U} · Enchantment · ROE #55 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** expensive spell-doubling setup. **Effect summary:** Seven-mana enchantment gives its controller's instants and sorceries rebound.

**Why this band:** Below target as an ordinary engine: spends seven mana and a card without changing the board, then requires another spell and time to realize the payoff.

**Costs and context:** A deck with abundant mana and strong spells can exploit it. The label is about its unsupported floor and deployment timetable.

## Artifact

### Too Strong: [Mana Crypt](https://scryfall.com/card/ema/225/mana-crypt)

**{0} · Artifact · EMA #225 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** zero-cost permanent fast mana. **Effect summary:** Zero-mana artifact taps for two colorless; upkeep coin flips can deal three damage to its controller.

**Why this band:** Above the ceiling: repeatable two-mana acceleration without a mana investment skips early curve stages. Random life loss does not reliably repay that tempo advantage.

**Costs and context:** Long games make the damage relevant. It still accelerates any deck with generic costs and needs very little synergy.

### Target: [Wurmcoil Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/som/223/wurmcoil-engine)

**{6}; 6/6 · Artifact Creature — Phyrexian Wurm · SOM #223 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** stabilizing resilient finisher. **Effect summary:** Six-mana 6/6 has deathtouch and lifelink; dying leaves two 3/3 tokens, each inheriting one ability.

**Why this band:** A strong mythic can dominate combat, stabilize life, and leave material after destruction. That is a suitable payoff for six mana in a powerful environment.

**Costs and context:** Colorless access makes this a high-end target, not an average six-drop. Exile, bounce, theft, and artifact answers differ from ordinary destruction.

### Too Weak: [Alhammarret's Archive](https://scryfall.com/card/ori/221/alhammarrets-archive)

**{5} · Legendary Artifact · ORI #221 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** resource-doubling engine. **Effect summary:** Five-mana artifact doubles life gain and most card draws, excluding the first draw in the draw step.

**Why this band:** Below target without a substantial support package: no body, entry value, or benefit to the ordinary first draw. Another effect must produce the resources it doubles.

**Costs and context:** With many repeatable draw or life-gain effects it can become powerful. Count those sources before assigning either verdict.

## Land

### Too Strong: [Tolarian Academy](https://scryfall.com/card/vma/319/tolarian-academy)

**Land — no mana cost · Legendary Land · VMA #319 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** artifact-funded fast mana land. **Effect summary:** A legendary land taps for blue mana equal to the number of controlled artifacts.

**Why this band:** Above the ceiling with cheap artifacts: a land drop produces multiple mana without an activation investment. Artifact tokens can accelerate the engine further.

**Costs and context:** Conditional ceiling: it produces nothing without artifacts. Rare and mythic printings are both shown; neither rarity makes a dense artifact shell safe.

### Target: [Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls)

**Land — no mana cost · Land · LCI #269 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** typed creature fixing. **Effect summary:** A land names a creature type; it produces colorless normally or any color for that type's creatures, which become uncounterable.

**Why this band:** A useful mythic land reference when tribes overlap: untapped fixing plus specialized protection without producing extra mana or winning by itself.

**Costs and context:** Only the colored-mana mode carries the creature-type restriction and uncounterability. It cannot pay arbitrary colored noncreature costs; no supported tribe means this is the wrong target.

### Too Weak: [Maze's End](https://scryfall.com/card/dgm/152/mazes-end)

**Land — no mana cost · Land · DGM #152 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** Gate engine and alternate win. **Effect summary:** A tapped land produces colorless; a three-mana activation returns it to hand, searches for a Gate, and checks for ten differently named Gates.

**Why this band:** Too weak in our current unsupported Gate context: slow fixing and an unreachable alternate victory do not justify a mythic slot.

**Costs and context:** A deliberate ten-Gate environment changes the verdict. It is a support-mismatch example, not evidence that alternate-win lands must always be weak.
