# Uncommon power references

[Guide and methodology](../10-modern-horizons-power.md) · [Machine-readable data](cards.json)

Fetched 2026-10-16. Bands are editorial judgments for the specified context. The card links establish printed facts, not our classification. Read the full costs and support requirements before applying a verdict.

## Creature

### Too Strong: [Amped Raptor](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/114/amped-raptor)

**{1}{R}; 2/1 · Creature — Dinosaur · MH3 #114 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** cheap body plus spell acceleration. **Effect summary:** A two-mana 2/1 first striker supplies energy and, when cast from hand, can use energy to cast the next revealed nonland card.

**Why this band:** Conditional upper boundary: with many cheap hits or surplus energy it bundles a creature, resource production, and a free spell into an explosive early turn. Do not reproduce reliable high-end hits for two mana.

**Costs and context:** In an ordinary draft curve it can miss on an unaffordable spell. Its Modern ban is corroboration of an optimized energy shell, not proof that every Limited copy is excessive.

**Additional context:** [source 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-december-16-2024). These sources do not establish an objective rating for this custom format.

### Target: [Flametongue Yearling](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/125/flametongue-yearling)

**{R}{R}; 2/1 · Creature — Kavu · MH2 #125 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** scalable removal creature. **Effect summary:** Two red mana buys a 2/1 that deals its power to a creature on entry; each two-mana kicker adds a counter.

**Why this band:** A real uncommon power anchor: body plus removal at two mana, with scaling when drawn late. Strong two-for-ones belong in the target environment.

**Costs and context:** Double red constrains casting. The damage target is mandatory and can be your own creature if no opponent creature is available.

### Too Weak: [Air Elemental](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/42/air-elemental)

**{3}{U}{U}; 4/4 · Creature — Elemental · M10 #42 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** evasive finisher. **Effect summary:** Five mana buys a 4/4 flyer with no other ability.

**Why this band:** Below the intended premium uncommon finisher: it does not refund a card, answer a threat, or protect the investment. This is a useful near-floor comparison for bland expensive flyers.

**Costs and context:** Flying still wins games. In a slower or flyer-light environment it can be good; the verdict concerns our stronger target.

## Instant

### Too Strong: [Mana Drain](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/65/mana-drain)

**{U}{U} · Instant · LEG #65 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** counterspell plus mana swing. **Effect summary:** A two-blue counterspell supplies colorless mana equal to the countered spell's mana value at the next main phase.

**Why this band:** Above the desired interaction ceiling: a full answer also finances a much earlier threat. Increasing rarity changes frequency but does not remove the tempo swing.

**Costs and context:** Requires a relevant opposing spell, double blue, and a way to use the temporary colorless mana. Its uncommon and mythic printings demonstrate why rarity alone is not a power scale.

### Target: [Counterspell](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/267/counterspell)

**{U}{U} · Instant · MH2 #267 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** broad stack interaction. **Effect summary:** Two blue mana counters a spell without a type restriction.

**Why this band:** This is a fair high-powered uncommon answer. Full interaction at two mana is allowed; do not add a weak tax or a four-mana price to make it feel safe.

**Costs and context:** Needs double blue and mana held open; cannot remove a permanent already in play.

### Too Weak: [Frazzle](https://scryfall.com/card/gpt/25/frazzle)

**{3}{U} · Instant · GPT #25 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** restricted counterspell. **Effect summary:** Four mana counters a nonblue spell.

**Why this band:** A four-mana answer with a color exclusion loses the key efficiency that makes countermagic useful. Its restriction does not buy a compensating upside.

**Costs and context:** Blue-light opponents improve coverage, but not its tempo cost.

## Sorcery

### Too Strong: [Channel](https://scryfall.com/card/4ed/236/channel)

**{G}{G} · Sorcery · 4ED #236 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** life-funded fast mana. **Effect summary:** Two green mana enables paying life for colorless mana during the turn.

**Why this band:** Above the upper bound with expensive generic-cost payoffs: it can bypass most of the mana curve immediately. Moving it from uncommon to mythic does not make that play pattern fair.

**Costs and context:** Requires payoff cards and enough life. An empty hand or colored-only costs limit it; do not invent a combo without identifying the payoff.

### Target: [Lingering Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/dka/12/lingering-souls)

**{2}{W} · Sorcery · DKA #12 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** resilient token pressure. **Effect summary:** Three mana creates two flying Spirits; a later two-mana flashback makes two more.

**Why this band:** A strong uncommon provides an independently useful first cast and a meaningful second resource. Two evasive bodies now and two later is the right ambition for token pressure.

**Costs and context:** Full output costs five mana across two casts and access to black for flashback. Graveyard hate and sweepers remain relevant.

### Too Weak: [Ransack](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/39/ransack)

**{3}{U} · Sorcery · STH #39 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** library manipulation. **Effect summary:** Four mana rearranges the top five cards of a chosen player’s library between top and bottom.

**Why this band:** Spends a card and a full midgame turn without drawing, answering a permanent, or creating a body. Selection needs a better rate or actual resource return.

**Costs and context:** Can manipulate an opponent’s draws or support top-deck payoffs; those uses must justify losing a card and tempo.

## Enchantment

### Too Strong: [Land Tax](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/26/land-tax)

**{W} · Enchantment · LEG #26 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** repeatable land-card advantage. **Effect summary:** A one-mana enchantment can repeatedly find up to three basic lands at upkeep when an opponent has more lands.

**Why this band:** Conditional upper boundary for cheap engines: repeated three-card refills can secure every land drop and fuel discard or hand-conversion effects with minimal mana investment.

**Costs and context:** It finds lands into hand, not the battlefield. Needs the land-count condition, basics, and preferably a way to use excess lands; it can be inactive when ahead.

### Target: [Goblin Bombardment](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/179/goblin-bombardment)

**{1}{R} · Enchantment · TMP #179 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** sacrifice outlet and reach. **Effect summary:** Two-mana enchantment converts sacrificed creatures into damage to any target without a mana activation cost.

**Why this band:** A strong, desirable build-around: every activation consumes a real creature. Free activation alone is not a reason to nerf a finite-resource engine.

**Costs and context:** Check token density and recursion. If a concrete cheap loop produces unlimited creatures, the package can cross the ceiling.

### Too Weak: [Honden of Cleansing Fire](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/14/honden-of-cleansing-fire)

**{3}{W} · Legendary Enchantment — Shrine · CHK #14 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** upkeep life-gain engine. **Effect summary:** Four-mana Shrine gains two life per controlled Shrine at upkeep.

**Why this band:** As a lone engine, it has no immediate board impact and only a small recurring reward. This is the same failure mode as the generated four-mana life-gain enchantment.

**Costs and context:** Shrine density changes the evaluation substantially. This weak verdict assumes no dedicated multi-Shrine package.

## Artifact

### Too Strong: [Skullclamp](https://scryfall.com/card/dst/140/skullclamp)

**{1} · Artifact — Equipment · DST #140 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** equipment card-draw engine. **Effect summary:** One-mana Equipment gives a power bonus and toughness penalty; equipped creatures dying draw two cards. Equip costs one.

**Why this band:** Above the ceiling: one-toughness tokens become very cheap two-card refills, and normal removal also feeds the engine. Common fodder makes this accessible without elaborate setup.

**Costs and context:** Count one mana per equip and a consumed creature. Those costs remain far too small when tokens are abundant.

### Target: [Barbed Spike](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/5/barbed-spike)

**{1}{W} · Artifact — Equipment · MH2 #5 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** equipment with an immediate flyer. **Effect summary:** Two-mana Equipment arrives attached to a 1/1 flying Thopter, adds one power, and later equips for two.

**Why this band:** A two-mana 2/1 flyer up front plus reusable equipment later is an excellent uncommon baseline. The first body pays for the development turn.

**Costs and context:** The token and Equipment are separate permanents; count both artifact entry triggers, but do not double-count the same creature's power.

### Too Weak: [Angel's Feather](https://scryfall.com/card/dst/92/angels-feather)

**{2} · Artifact · DST #92 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** conditional life-gain artifact. **Effect summary:** Two-mana artifact gains one life when a player casts a white spell.

**Why this band:** No board effect, card return, or serious payoff for its setup. Increasing the number of trigger opportunities does not automatically make a weak reward worthwhile.

**Costs and context:** Life-gain payoffs can help, but prefer enablers that also attack, block, or replace themselves.

## Land

### Too Strong: [Strip Mine](https://scryfall.com/card/4ed/363/strip-mine)

**Land — no mana cost · Land · 4ED #363 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** land denial. **Effect summary:** A land produces colorless mana or sacrifices itself to destroy any land.

**Why this band:** Above our utility-land ceiling: a land slot can erase an opponent's only basic of a color, and recursion can lock development. Basics do not provide a safe escape.

**Costs and context:** One-for-one land trading has a real cost. Recursion and low-land aggressive decks make it most dangerous; the verdict is about the desired play experience as well as efficiency.

### Target: [Ghost Quarter](https://scryfall.com/card/dis/173/ghost-quarter)

**Land — no mana cost · Land · DIS #173 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** answer to exceptional lands. **Effect summary:** A colorless land can sacrifice to destroy a land, whose controller may find a basic replacement.

**Why this band:** A reasonable safety valve: it answers a problematic land while usually preserving the opponent's ability to play. The replacement is an intentional cost, not useless generosity.

**Costs and context:** Needs actual troublesome lands to justify the slot. Repeated activations can exhaust basics; inspect recursion and basic counts.

### Too Weak: [Terminal Moraine](https://scryfall.com/card/pls/142/terminal-moraine)

**Land — no mana cost · Land · PLS #142 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** slow basic-land fixing. **Effect summary:** A colorless land requires two mana, tapping, and sacrifice to find a tapped basic.

**Why this band:** Below the fixing target because the conversion ties up the land plus two other mana and delays the replacement. Low immediate cost hides substantial tempo loss.

**Costs and context:** It offers late flexibility. Its weakness is the full conversion cost, not an inability to produce mana.
