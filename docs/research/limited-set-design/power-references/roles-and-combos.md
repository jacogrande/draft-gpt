# Additional role anchors and a concrete combo warning

[Guide and methodology](../10-modern-horizons-power.md) · [Machine-readable data](cards.json)

The broad type/rarity grid is not enough to compare an Equipment, a mana rock, and a cantrip artifact. These references supply closer functional matches. They are independent reference options, not a recommended list of cards to combine in one environment.

### Supplemental Target: [Refurbished Familiar](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/105/refurbished-familiar)

**{3}{B}; 2/1 · Artifact Creature — Zombie Rat · MH3 #105 · printed common.**

**Role:** affinity value creature. **Effect summary:** Four-mana 2/1 flyer with artifact discounts and an entry discard effect that draws instead when the opponent cannot discard.

**Why this band:** A cheap supported common can supply evasion and immediate card advantage. Compare the expected discounted cost, not just the printed four.

**Costs and context:** Strong target only with declared artifact density. Artifact lands and plentiful tokens can make one-mana casts routine; check that ceiling.

### Supplemental Target: [Thraben Charm](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/45/thraben-charm)

**{1}{W} · Instant · MH3 #45 · printed common.**

**Role:** flexible common interaction. **Effect summary:** Two-mana instant chooses creature-count damage, enchantment destruction, or graveyard exile.

**Why this band:** Modes make an answer relevant in more matchups. Common interaction can be flexible and efficient without needing extra drawback clauses.

**Costs and context:** The damage mode depends on your board and can fail to kill through removal. The other modes do not require creatures.

### Supplemental Target: [Unholy Heat](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/145/unholy-heat)

**{R} · Instant · MH2 #145 · printed common.**

**Role:** cheap removal with supported scaling. **Effect summary:** One red mana deals two damage to a creature or planeswalker, increasing to six with four graveyard card types.

**Why this band:** Two damage is already useful. Ordinary deck construction can unlock a major payoff, instead of unlocking mere playability.

**Costs and context:** Delirium requires actual type diversity and graveyard access. Do not assume six damage is always available on turn one.

### Supplemental Target: [Thraben Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/soi/44/thraben-inspector)

**{W}; 1/2 · Creature — Human Soldier · SOI #44 · printed common.**

**Role:** one-drop body plus deferred card. **Effect summary:** One white mana makes a 1/2 and a Clue.

**Why this band:** An inexpensive enabler should leave material immediately and a useful later option. This is a good floor for small value creatures.

**Costs and context:** The Clue costs two more mana to draw. It is not a free immediate cantrip; artifact and token synergies add separate value.

### Supplemental Target: [Faithless Looting](https://scryfall.com/card/dka/87/faithless-looting)

**{R} · Sorcery · DKA #87 · printed common.**

**Role:** cheap selection and graveyard setup. **Effect summary:** One red mana draws two and discards two; a three-mana flashback repeats it.

**Why this band:** Card disadvantage can be worthwhile when the spell is very cheap and actively enables graveyard plans. Do not ban every draw/discard pattern after the weak two-mana sample.

**Costs and context:** The first cast loses one net card. It needs valuable selection or discarded-card use, and flashback costs real mana.

### Supplemental Target: [Abiding Grace](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/1/abiding-grace)

**{2}{W} · Enchantment · MH2 #1 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** repeatable small-creature recovery. **Effect summary:** Three-mana enchantment offers one life or returns a one-mana creature at each of your end steps.

**Why this band:** This is a substantial engine that can pay off on the casting turn. The attractive mode returns a real creature, not just a little life.

**Costs and context:** Requires enough one-drops, trades, and sacrifice outlets. Judge repeated entry/death effects; the life mode is the fallback.

### Supplemental Target: [Static Prison](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/44/static-prison)

**{W} · Enchantment · MH3 #44 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** cheap broad temporary removal. **Effect summary:** One-mana enchantment exiles an opposing nonland permanent, supplies two energy, and needs energy at each first main phase to remain.

**Why this band:** A one-mana broad answer can be strong with a genuine duration constraint. The card comes with its first maintenance payments.

**Costs and context:** Additional energy extends the removal. Without it, the threat returns; counting this as permanent unconditional exile misprices it.

### Supplemental Target: [Persist](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/96/persist)

**{1}{B} · Sorcery · MH2 #96 · printed rare.**

**Role:** efficient graveyard-to-battlefield recovery. **Effect summary:** Two mana reanimates a nonlegendary creature with a minus-one counter.

**Why this band:** High-powered recovery can return a meaningful body directly at an aggressive rate. Five mana merely to put an artifact into hand is nowhere close.

**Costs and context:** Requires a creature in the graveyard. Large entry effects such as Archon of Cruelty change the acceptable package; this is not permission for arbitrary early cheat targets.

### Supplemental Target: [Tireless Tracker](https://scryfall.com/card/soi/233/tireless-tracker)

**{2}{G}; 3/2 · Creature — Human Scout · SOI #233 · printed rare.**

**Role:** midgame value threat. **Effect summary:** Three-mana 3/2 makes Clues from landfall and gains counters when Clues are sacrificed.

**Why this band:** A threat can link routine land drops, delayed cards, and permanent growth. This is a coherent rare engine rather than a weak body with unrelated flavor text.

**Costs and context:** No guaranteed entry Clue unless a land can follow it. Each ordinary Clue activation costs two; do not count all future cards as immediate.

### Supplemental Target: [Solitude](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/32/solitude)

**{3}{W}{W}; 3/2 · Creature — Elemental Incarnation · MH2 #32 · printed mythic.**

**Role:** removal creature with a costly free mode. **Effect summary:** Five-mana 3/2 with flash and lifelink exiles another creature for life compensation; pitching a white card can evoke it.

**Why this band:** Efficient interaction attached to a useful body is appropriate at mythic. A free defensive mode may deliberately trade card economy for tempo.

**Costs and context:** Evoke normally sacrifices Solitude, so pitching spends two cards for the removal. Blink can evade that sacrifice; inspect the exact package before approving it.

### Supplemental Target: [Ornithopter of Paradise](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/232/ornithopter-of-paradise)

**{2}; 0/2 · Artifact Creature — Thopter · MH2 #232 · printed common.**

**Role:** cheap fixing creature. **Effect summary:** Two-mana 0/2 artifact flyer taps for any color.

**Why this band:** Two mana can provide both a blocker and flexible acceleration. Creature vulnerability and summoning sickness are meaningful costs, not reasons to add more taxes.

**Costs and context:** It cannot tap for mana immediately without haste. Artifact synergies improve it; board wipes also remove the acceleration.

### Supplemental Target: [Worn Powerstone](https://scryfall.com/card/usg/318/worn-powerstone)

**{3} · Artifact · USG #318 · printed uncommon.**

**Role:** substantial mana acceleration. **Effect summary:** Three-mana artifact enters tapped and later produces two colorless mana per tap.

**Why this band:** A three-mana ramp investment should create a meaningful jump. Compare a rock’s future output and entry delay with its casting cost.

**Costs and context:** No fixing and no mana on the entry turn. Multiple copies or untap effects require reviewing how early expensive threats arrive.

### Supplemental Target: [Bonesplitter](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/146/bonesplitter)

**{1} · Artifact — Equipment · MRD #146 · printed common.**

**Role:** efficient combat Equipment. **Effect summary:** One-mana Equipment adds two power and equips for one.

**Why this band:** A small combat bonus needs a small combined investment: two total mana for the first equipped attacker, then one to move it.

**Costs and context:** Supplies no body or card replacement. Evasion and token density matter, and removing the carrier can create a tempo loss.

### Combo Warning: [Basking Broodscale](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/145/basking-broodscale)

**{1}{G}; 2/2 · Creature — Eldrazi Lizard · MH3 #145 · printed common.**

**Role:** counter engine and combo component. **Effect summary:** A two-mana 2/2 can adapt for two mana and makes a Spawn whenever it receives one or more counters.

**Why this band:** An appealing supported common on its own, but it becomes a concrete unlimited loop with Sadistic Glee. This is a package warning, not a blanket ban on counters.

**Costs and context:** With Glee attached, sacrifice a Spawn: Glee adds a counter to Broodscale, which makes the next Spawn. Each repetition produces colorless mana; an initial Spawn or paid adapt starts it.

### Combo Warning: [Sadistic Glee](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/153/sadistic-glee)

**{B} · Enchantment — Aura · TMP #153 · printed common.**

**Role:** death-trigger Aura and combo component. **Effect summary:** One-mana Aura gives its enchanted creature a counter whenever a creature dies.

**Why this band:** Cheap individually modest enablers can remove the limits on another card. Review the loop with Broodscale rather than adding arbitrary once-per-turn text to every sacrifice card.

**Costs and context:** Needs Broodscale, the Aura, and a starting death or Spawn; removal can disrupt the chain. Unlimited colorless mana needs a sink to win immediately, while the creature also grows without bound.
