Fetched 2026-10-16. Bands are editorial judgments for the specified context. The card links establish printed facts, not our classification. Read the full costs and support requirements before applying a verdict.

## Creature

### Too Strong: [Writhing Chrysalis](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/208/writhing-chrysalis)

**{2}{R}{G}; 2/3 · Creature — Eldrazi Drone · MH3 #208 · printed common.**

**Role:** midgame board, ramp, sacrifice payoff. **Effect summary:** Four mana buys a reach body, two Spawn on casting, and permanent growth whenever another Eldrazi is sacrificed.

**Why this band:** Above the desired common ceiling: it supplies its own fuel, grows while refunding mana, and improves sharply in multiples. Treat this as a common outlier to avoid normalizing, not the baseline for every four-drop.

**Costs and context:** Eldrazi/Spawn support makes the ceiling higher; the two included Spawn already turn it into a 4/5 and generate two mana when sacrificed.

**Additional context:** [source 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=214jwV92jcY). These sources do not establish an objective rating for this custom format.

### Target: [Eldrazi Repurposer](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/150/eldrazi-repurposer)

**{2}{G}; 3/3 · Creature — Eldrazi Drone · MH3 #150 · printed common.**

**Role:** midgame creature and ramp fodder. **Effect summary:** A three-mana 3/3 makes one Spawn when cast and another when it dies.

**Why this band:** A useful body plus immediate and deferred resources is the desired common floor for a supported midgame role. There is value even when the main creature trades.

**Costs and context:** Spawn sacrifice, ramp, and death synergies add value. The cast trigger also supplies a Spawn if the creature spell is countered.

### Too Weak: [Hill Giant](https://scryfall.com/card/9ed/197/hill-giant)

**{3}{R}; 3/3 · Creature — Giant · 9ED #197 · printed common.**

**Role:** midgame attacker. **Effect summary:** Four mana for a vanilla 3/3.

**Why this band:** Below the target: no immediate resource, evasion, resilience, or synergy compensates for spending a midgame turn. This is a deliberately obvious lower bound.

**Costs and context:** Creature-type bonuses could improve it, but do not justify this baseline in an ordinary supported deck.

## Instant

### Too Strong: [Sprout Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/fut/138/sprout-swarm)

**{1}{G} · Instant · FUT #138 · printed common.**

**Role:** repeatable token engine. **Effect summary:** Creates a Saproling; convoke helps pay its two-mana cost and optional three-mana buyback.

**Why this band:** Too strong as a frequent common in a creature-rich environment: the output helps fund later casts while buyback preserves the spell. It can take over stalled games without drawing more engine pieces.

**Costs and context:** Conditional ceiling: needs bodies or spare mana and is vulnerable to counters and board resets. It is not automatically infinite.

### Target: [Galvanic Discharge](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/122/galvanic-discharge)

**{R} · Instant · MH3 #122 · printed common.**

**Role:** cheap creature interaction. **Effect summary:** One red mana supplies three energy, then spends any amount of available energy as damage to a creature or planeswalker.

**Why this band:** This is the desired efficiency: three damage for one mana without needing prior setup, with a relevant resource interface. Do not weaken it just because it is common.

**Costs and context:** Additional energy can increase damage; spending less can bank energy. It cannot damage players.

### Too Weak: [Healing Salve](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/18/healing-salve)

**{W} · Instant · 7ED #18 · printed common.**

**Role:** protection or life buffer. **Effect summary:** One white mana either gains three life or prevents the next three damage to a target that turn.

**Why this band:** Usually too little lasting value for spending a card. A dedicated combat save can work, but the generic common slot should offer broader protection, replacement, or offensive use.

**Costs and context:** Damage-based combat can make prevention useful; this is a weak default, not a claim it never wins a combat.

## Sorcery

### Too Strong: [Treasure Cruise](https://scryfall.com/card/ktk/59/treasure-cruise)

**{7}{U} · Sorcery · KTK #59 · printed common.**

**Role:** graveyard-funded card draw. **Effect summary:** Draws three cards; graveyard cards can pay the seven generic mana through delve.

**Why this band:** Too strong when routine play reliably makes this cost one blue mana: a large resource refill with almost no tempo loss. The printed eight mana is not its effective price in a stocked graveyard.

**Costs and context:** Conditional ceiling: without self-mill, cheap spells, or a developed graveyard it is expensive. Delve consumes resources other cards may need.

### Target: [Malevolent Rumble](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/161/malevolent-rumble)

**{1}{G} · Sorcery · MH3 #161 · printed common.**

**Role:** selection, graveyard setup, ramp. **Effect summary:** For two mana, selects a permanent from the top four, mills the rest, and creates a Spawn.

**Why this band:** A strong common should perform useful jobs together: find a needed resource, stock the graveyard, and leave a body or future mana. This is a concrete generation anchor.

**Costs and context:** Can miss on a permanent-light deck. Permanent density and graveyard synergies matter; this is not unconditional draw-two.

### Too Weak: [Divination](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/49/divination)

**{2}{U} · Sorcery · M10 #49 · printed common.**

**Role:** straight card draw. **Effect summary:** Three mana at sorcery speed draws two cards.

**Why this band:** Below our premium spell-slot target: it replaces itself and gains one card but supplies no board, selection, discount, or later reuse. Prefer a meaningful second job at this cost.

**Costs and context:** Still playable when the format is slow or card draw is scarce. A near-floor reference, not an intrinsically bad Magic card.

## Enchantment

### Too Strong: [Pestilence](https://scryfall.com/card/usg/147/pestilence)

**{2}{B}{B} · Enchantment · USG #147 · printed common.**

**Role:** repeatable sweeper and reach. **Effect summary:** A four-mana enchantment converts each black mana into damage to every creature and player; it expires if the battlefield has no creatures at the end step.

**Why this band:** Above the common ceiling in a small-creature format: repeatable board control and player damage concentrate too much of the game in one widely available engine.

**Costs and context:** Needs black mana, life to spend, and often a durable creature. High toughness, enchantment removal, and the end-step condition provide counterplay.

### Target: [Witness Protection](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/66/witness-protection)

**{U} · Enchantment — Aura · SNC #66 · printed common.**

**Role:** cheap creature neutralization. **Effect summary:** One-mana Aura removes a creature's abilities and makes it a 1/1.

**Why this band:** Appropriate strong common interaction: a tiny mana commitment substantially answers a threat while leaving the opponent a body and possible escape routes.

**Costs and context:** It is not hard removal. Enchantment removal, bounce, and sacrifice can undo or exploit the remaining creature.

### Too Weak: [Asha's Favor](https://scryfall.com/card/con/2/ashas-favor)

**{2}{W} · Enchantment — Aura · CON #2 · printed common.**

**Role:** combat Aura. **Effect summary:** Three-mana Aura grants flying, first strike, and vigilance without increasing size.

**Why this band:** Too much card and tempo exposure for an enhancement with no replacement or immediate body. A removal response can erase the investment.

**Costs and context:** A protected large creature or Aura-specific deck improves it; assess those supports rather than assuming every keyword package is valuable.

## Artifact

### Too Strong: [Cranial Plating](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/113/cranial-plating)

**{2} · Artifact — Equipment · 5DN #113 · printed common.**

**Role:** artifact combat payoff. **Effect summary:** Two-mana Equipment adds power equal to artifact count; equip costs one, with a two-black instant attachment option.

**Why this band:** Above our common payoff ceiling when artifacts are plentiful: it translates cheap permanents and artifact lands into large evasive damage at very low attachment cost.

**Costs and context:** Conditional ceiling: count actual artifacts, evasive carriers, and removal. A low-artifact set should not reject its descendants by name alone.

### Target: [Lembas](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/243/lembas)

**{2} · Artifact — Food · LTR #243 · printed common.**

**Role:** artifact cantrip and sacrifice resource. **Effect summary:** Two mana gives scry and a card immediately; later two mana and sacrifice gain life, and the artifact shuffles back when it dies.

**Why this band:** A useful artifact replaces itself before asking for synergy. The later sacrifice is optional utility, not the sole reward for spending the original card.

**Costs and context:** The later life costs two more mana. Artifact entry, sacrifice, and Food support raise its value; shuffling it back is not an immediate extra card.

### Too Weak: [Golden Urn](https://scryfall.com/card/som/158/golden-urn)

**{1} · Artifact · SOM #158 · printed common.**

**Role:** delayed life-gain artifact. **Effect summary:** One-mana artifact accumulates upkeep counters and can be tapped and sacrificed for that much life.

**Why this band:** Spends a card for slow life gain without affecting the board or replacing itself. Cheapness alone does not rescue an effect with so little return.

**Costs and context:** Artifact-count support supplies some value, but stronger cantrip artifacts can usually fill that role.

## Land

### Too Strong: [Seat of the Synod](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/283/seat-of-the-synod)

**Land — no mana cost · Artifact Land · MRD #283 · printed common.**

**Role:** untapped artifact mana source. **Effect summary:** An artifact land that enters untapped and produces blue mana.

**Why this band:** Too efficient when artifact count is a major resource: a normal land drop also discounts affinity, grows payoffs, and supplies sacrifice material without a tempo charge.

**Costs and context:** Conditional ceiling: ordinary in decks without artifact rewards; vulnerable to artifact removal. Compare with the tapped Bridge, not with a spell that consumes mana.

### Target: [Silverbluff Bridge](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/255/silverbluff-bridge)

**Land — no mana cost · Artifact Land · MH2 #255 · printed common.**

**Role:** two-color fixing and artifact support. **Effect summary:** An indestructible artifact land that enters tapped and produces blue or red.

**Why this band:** A good common synergy land: fixes two colors and supports artifacts, with a real entry-tempo cost. The cost keeps the extra resource type from being free.

**Costs and context:** Still inspect affinity and indestructible interactions. Strong common fixing is not license to make every land untapped.

### Too Weak: [Shimmering Grotto](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/273/shimmering-grotto)

**Land — no mana cost · Land · LRW #273 · printed common.**

**Role:** rainbow filtering. **Effect summary:** Produces colorless normally; producing a chosen color requires paying an additional mana.

**Why this band:** Below the intended routine fixing floor: each colored activation ties up an extra mana source and disrupts the curve. It can rescue a splash but should not be the premium fixer.

**Costs and context:** Five-color flexibility has value; compare total mana available after filtering, not just number of colors listed.
