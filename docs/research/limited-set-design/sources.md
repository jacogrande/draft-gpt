# Sources and evidence notes

[Research index](README.md)

Accessed 2026-10-14. All 39 external sources cited in this directory are published by Wizards of the Coast. Years below refer to publication, not the age of the design being discussed. A handoff published during previews may describe decisions made years earlier.

The research uses public article text and substantive passages returned through web search/open. It does not rely on Reddit commentary, private design documents, unpublished playtest data, or podcast transcripts. Links sit beside claims in the chapters; this catalogue provides the consolidated reading list.

## Limits of this research

- The source selection favors explicit explanations of design decisions. It is not a complete history of every Limited format.
- Designer accounts describe intent and reported experience. They do not expose the complete internal evidence behind every choice.
- Release guides explain intended decks; they are not post-release statistical evaluations.
- Older skeletons, mechanic ratings, and color-pie entries must retain their dates. Later products can deliberately depart from them.
- Source text occasionally contains errors. The apparent Squirrel color typo in Bloomburrow's handoff is cross-checked against the released guide in chapter 6.
- The numerical examples in chapter 3 and table-size calculations in chapter 7 are original, labeled calculations. They are not Wizards targets.
- Repository observations are linked directly to code and dated. Proposed changes remain research conclusions until a separate specification adopts them.

Future research should test generated card pools and actual play. No amount of additional article reading can establish that a particular DraftGPT environment is balanced.
